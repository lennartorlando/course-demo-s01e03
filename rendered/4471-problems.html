The problems

parser, ticket #1. The shared parser reads dates month first, and every tenant in #4471 writes the day first. “Our 7 April reminder went out on 4 July.” Every channel, every tenant.

hint, ticket #2. The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People follow the hint, and the form reads what they typed the tenant’s way, day first. Only the form, only booking-v3 tenants. It looks like the parser bug, and a parser fix leaves it as it is.

timing, ticket #3. No month flipped: “Invite says Tuesday 12 May. Calendar says Monday 11 May.” “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.” Most likely a date converted between time zones. Not reproduced yet; the ticket asks the reporters for times and zones.

How the reports were split

  1. One report per id: the export pages overlap, so some reports come twice.
  2. The desk’s reading first: reports in desk-notes.json read as a one-day move are timing.
  3. Then the cause, by tenant and channel (causes.ts): a form report at a booking-v3 tenant is hint, any other report from a day-first tenant is parser.

The intake tag is not used. It guesses from the reporter’s browser, and for these reports it is wrong often enough to split them the wrong way.

Why one ticket held three

The desk merges on subject alone (decision 0001). All three problems arrive as “wrong day on the invite”, so they met in one ticket. The #4471 page shows the split already: its table puts the hint reports in a row of their own, and a footnote says the one-day moves are counted under parser. Its headline still says one issue.
